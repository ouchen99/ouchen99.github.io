Privacy Policy — Shift Calendar: Hours & Pay

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Shift Calendar: Hours & Pay helps you track rotating work shifts, logged hours, overtime, and estimated pay. We designed the app so your personal work schedule and pay records stay private on your phone.

1. Your Schedule and Pay Data Stays on Your Device

No account or sign-up is required to use the app. All data you enter — including jobs, shift rotations, calendar overrides, logged hours, hourly rates, notes, exports, and backup files — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your schedule or pay information. If you export a CSV, PDF, or JSON backup file, it is saved or shared only where you choose using Android's standard share or file picker.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show, personalize (where permitted), cap frequency, combat fraud, and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including your device's Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • App usage, ad interaction, and diagnostic / crash performance data

For details on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please read: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

3. Consent Choices (EEA, UK, and Applicable Regions)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), Switzerland, or another region requiring consent, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your ad consent choices before loading ads. Where required, you can review or change your consent preferences at any time in the app's Settings (under Privacy options) and you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

4. Permissions

The app uses internet access solely to request consent forms and load ads, and optional notification permissions to show local shift reminders and your active clock-in timer on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Shift Calendar: Hours & Pay is intended for working adults (18+) and is not directed to children under 13.
